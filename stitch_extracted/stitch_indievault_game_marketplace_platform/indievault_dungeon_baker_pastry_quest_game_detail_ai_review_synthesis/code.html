local_cafe About the Game

Published by Independent

Welcome to The Flourishing Hearth, the deepest and warmest dungeon tavern in the Whispering Depths. As Lyra, an apprentice baker imbued with ancient sourdough enchantment, you’ll transform wild dungeon flora, cave-gathered crystal yeasts, and solar-milled wheat into mouthwatering delicacies for battle-weary adventurers.

Balance the chaotic rush of paladins demanding brioche shields, rogues ordering stealth cinnamon twists, and wizard guilds bargaining for mana-infused croissants before they delve into the next floor.

bakery_dining 50+ Magical Recipes

Experiment with yeast starters that react to real dungeon weather and heat modifiers from the primordial kiln.

group Procedural Adventurer Parties

Every adventuring clan brings distinct culinary cravings and unlocks rare dungeon seeds for your cellar.

Over 90 minutes of relaxing acoustic medieval compositions by the Croissant Coven in-house composer.
